Welcome Home

More than a building — a family

Jesus Heals Fellowship Church is a fellowship community in Ambati Satram Area, Vizianagaram. Built on grace, kindness, and hope — where faith feels real and people feel known.

You don't have to have it all together.

Jesus Heals Fellowship Church is a fellowship community for real people with real questions and real lives. Whether you've been a believer your whole life or you're just curious, you'll find open doors, warm faces, and a message of hope that speaks to where you are today.

We gather Fridays at 7:00 PM and Sundays at 10:00 AM to worship, to learn, and to simply be together. No pressure. No pretense. Just grace.

"Come to me, all who are weary and burdened, and I will give you rest." — Matthew 11:28

First time here?

Here's what to expect when you walk through our doors:

  • A warm and welcoming environment
  • Worship and a message centered on faith and hope
  • An opportunity to connect with the church community
  • Time for fellowship and connection
